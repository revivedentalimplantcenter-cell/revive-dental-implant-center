Good to know

Questions, answered.

Are dental implants painful?Most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is. With modern anaesthesia and precise planning, implant placement typically feels like a simple filling — mild soreness for a day or two, managed with routine medication.
How long do implants last?With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants routinely last decades — often a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years of use.
Am I too old for braces or aligners?Not at all. We straighten smiles for teenagers, working professionals and patients in their 50s. Healthy gums and bone matter far more than age.
How many visits does a root canal need?Most root canals at Revive finish in a single visit using rotary endodontics. Complex cases may need two short sittings.
Is teeth whitening safe for enamel?Professional in-office whitening, done under supervision, is enamel-safe. We always check your teeth and gums first and customise the strength.
